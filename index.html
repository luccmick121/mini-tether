The Pitch

Why MUSDT exists

USDT is the world's most used stablecoin — but it wasn't designed for small payments. MUSDT is its little sibling: same DNA, optimized for micro-transactions.

💡

The Problem

Sending $0.50 worth of USDT on Ethereum often costs more in gas than the payment itself. Micro-transactions — tips, in-game purchases, content paywalls, API pay-per-call — are economically unfeasible with existing stablecoins.

🪙

The Solution

MUSDT tracks USDT 1:1 through Uniswap V3 concentrated liquidity. Holders get USDT-price exposure in a token designed from the ground up for small-value, high-frequency use cases.

🔓

Why It's Different

Zero-tax. No admin functions. No pause. No blacklist. No mint. Fixed supply forever. The contract is verified on Etherscan and immutable — what you read is what runs.

The Peg

1 MUSDT ≈ 1 USDT

Maintained by concentrated liquidity on Uniswap V3. When MUSDT trades above $1, arbitrageurs sell. When below, they buy. The peg is enforced by market mechanics — not by a centralized reserve.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is MUSDT backed by real USDT?
MUSDT is pegged to USDT via a Uniswap V3 concentrated liquidity pool. It is not a collateralized stablecoin — it's a synthetic tracking token. The peg is maintained by arbitrage mechanics, not by a reserve promise.
Why not just use USDT for micro-transactions?
You can — and should for larger amounts. MUSDT is specifically designed as a complementary token for the micro-transaction vertical: tipping, gaming, pay-per-use APIs, and content monetization where the branding, community, and tokenomics favor small-value high-frequency flows.
Is the contract safe?
The contract is a simplified mirror of the original TetherToken (same Solidity family, same ABI structure) with all administrative functions removed. No pause, no blacklist, no mint, no upgrade. It's verified on Etherscan — read the source yourself. The contract is immutable: what's deployed is what runs, forever.
Can the supply change?
No. The total supply of 100 billion MUSDT is fixed in the constructor. There is no mint function. The supply cannot increase, ever.
What keeps the price at $1?
A Uniswap V3 pool with concentrated liquidity around $1.00. When MUSDT drifts above $1.00, arbitrage bots sell MUSDT into the pool for profit, pushing the price down. When it drifts below, they buy, pushing it up. This is the same mechanism that keeps all AMM-pegged tokens near their target.
How is MUSDT distributed?
The entire supply is held in the project treasury. There was no presale, no team allocation, and no VC round. Distribution happens through the liquidity pool and community programs. All movements are visible on Etherscan.
What are the use cases?
Tipping: reward content creators with USDT-value.
Gaming: in-game currency backed by stablecoin value.
API & Pay-per-use: metered payments for decentralized services.
Content paywalls: micro-subscriptions and article unlocks.
Remittance: small-value cross-border transfers.
Is this a memecoin?
No — but it doesn't take itself too seriously either. MUSDT is a utility token with a clear purpose (micro-transactions), real infrastructure (verified contract, Uniswap pool), and transparent tokenomics. The mascot just makes it fun.

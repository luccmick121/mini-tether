Why another tether?

USDT is the dollar of on-chain finance, built for reserves, settlement and size. But most of what happens on-chain is small: a tip, a fraction of a cent per API call, a game item, a retry fee.

Mini Tether exists for that traffic. It is a mirror of USDT's unit (same value, same 6-decimal precision) inside a brand that belongs to the micro world, with a mascot, a fixed supply and nothing else attached.

Common questions

What is MUSDT?
Mini Tether (MUSDT) is a legitimate, independent ERC-20 token on Ethereum mainnet — a real, standalone currency built for micro-transactions and micropayments: tipping, gaming and pay-per-use services. It tracks USDT 1:1 through on-chain liquidity.
How does the peg work?
An on-chain liquidity pool trades the MUSDT/USDT pair. When MUSDT drifts above $1, arbitrageurs sell into the pool; when below, they buy. Standard market mechanics — there is no centralized reserve and no counterparty behind the peg.
Is the contract safe?
The source is verified on Etherscan and mirrors the original TetherToken surface with the administrative machinery removed: no mint, no pause, no blacklist, no upgrades. The only owner functions are cosmetic — name and symbol. Nothing in the code can touch balances. Read it yourself on Etherscan.
Why not just use USDT?
For reserves and large payments, you should use USDT. MUSDT is its micro companion: a legitimate token whose brand, mascot and precision are focused on the micro-transaction flows that big-money tooling was never designed around.
Can the supply change?
No. The 100 billion supply is set in the constructor and no mint function exists. Supply is fixed forever.
Do I need to connect a wallet here?
No. This site has no wallet integration at all. Copy the contract address and track everything on Etherscan, or import it into your own wallet app to watch the balance. Nothing here ever asks for a connection.

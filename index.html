FAQ

Common questions

What is MUSDT?+
MUSDT is a synthetic token that tracks USDT's price through a Uniswap V3 liquidity pool. It's designed for micro-transactions — small-value, high-frequency payments like tipping, gaming, and pay-per-use services.
How does the peg work?+
A concentrated liquidity pool on Uniswap V3 maintains the 1:1 relationship with USDT. When MUSDT drifts above $1, arbitrageurs sell into the pool. When below, they buy. Standard AMM market mechanics — no centralized reserve.
Is the contract safe?+
The contract mirrors the original TetherToken's code structure with all administrative functions removed: no pause, no blacklist, no mint, no upgrade path. It's verified on Etherscan and immutable.
Why not just use USDT?+
For larger amounts, you should use USDT. MUSDT is complementary — a token whose branding, mascot, and community focus on the micro-transaction vertical that USDT wasn't designed for.
Can the supply change?+
No. The 100 billion supply is set in the constructor. No mint function exists. Supply is fixed forever.
How do I get MUSDT?+
MUSDT is available on Ethereum. Import the contract address into your wallet to track your balance. Check Etherscan for current pool data and holder statistics.

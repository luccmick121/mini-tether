FAQ

Common questions

Is MUSDT backed by real USDT?
MUSDT is pegged to USDT via a Uniswap V3 concentrated liquidity pool. It's a synthetic tracking token — the peg is maintained by arbitrage mechanics, not a collateralized reserve.
Why not just use USDT?
You should for larger amounts. MUSDT is a complementary token for micro-transactions: tipping, gaming, pay-per-use APIs, and content monetization where branding and tokenomics favor small-value flows.
Is the contract safe?
The contract mirrors the original TetherToken structure with all admin functions removed: no pause, no blacklist, no mint, no upgrades. Verified on Etherscan. Immutable — what's deployed is what runs, forever.
Can the supply change?
No. 100 billion MUSDT is fixed in the constructor. No mint function exists. The supply cannot increase, ever.
What keeps it at $1?
Uniswap V3 concentrated liquidity around $1.00. Above $1, arbitrageurs sell (pushing down). Below $1, they buy (pushing up). Standard AMM peg mechanics.
What are the use cases?
Tipping streamers and creators · in-game currency with stablecoin value · metered API payments · content paywalls · micro-remittance · dApp economies.
Is this a memecoin?
No. MUSDT is a utility token with real infrastructure (verified contract, Uniswap pool, transparent tokenomics). The mascot just makes it fun.
